## 3. THE POSITIVE CONTROL — does `no-deferral` have any power at all?

either way the gate reads green. **Zero is the passing answer to every ban.** So the gate was tested
against a planted deferral in a real shipped source file, and the plant was grepped back *before*
believing anything — a plant that fails to plant is indistinguishable from a gate that cannot see.
