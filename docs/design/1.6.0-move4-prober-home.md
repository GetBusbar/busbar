# 1.6.0 MOVE 4 — the prober's home, measured at `28b0fbe46`

MOVE 4 (switch-over plan §5 row 10, −535) is the line that takes `busbar-llm`'s active health
prober — its loop, its state, its schedule — onto `busbar-unit-breaker` by identity, puts the llm
plane's probe face in `busbar-plane-llm` behind the contract's probe face type, and DELETES the
legacy loop in the same commit. The owner ruling in force is that active probing is a CORE
capability whose schedule and state live on the breaker unit, with one probe FACE per plane, nothing
plane-specific in a unit, and a unit that never names the substrate.

This note is the measurement that line asked for, made on this base and not inherited from the
earlier design. It does not move the prober. It says, with the rows, why the move cannot be made
here yet and exactly what makes it landable.

## 0. The finding in one line

**Two breaker books are live on this tip, and the served llm path does not read the unit's.** Moving
the schedule onto `busbar-unit-breaker` and deleting `engine/health.rs` in the same commit would
schedule probes against a book no llm request consults, and would switch active probing OFF for
served llm traffic. That is a capability loss dressed as a move, and the rules forbid both halves of
it: a move must be by identity, and legacy is deleted rather than left beside.

## 1. What is on this base

`crates/busbar-llm/src/engine/health.rs` — 535 lines, plus `tests/health_tests.rs` 684,
`tests/probe_guard_tests.rs` 119 and `tests/probe_release_owner_tests.rs` 241. It carries:

- `ProbeSchedule` — generation counter, a monotonic `origin`, one `AtomicU64` deadline per lane, the
  `UNSCHEDULED` sentinel, `advance_owned_deadline`'s compare-exchange and the spawn-time `fetch_min`
  (earliest-at-spawn, later-at-tick — the asymmetry is why one primitive cannot serve both);
- `spawn_probers(&Arc<dyn EngineHost>)` — one tokio task per lane with a `health:` mode, an
  `interval_at` ticker with `MissedTickBehavior::Skip`, the `Weak<dyn EngineHost>` upgrade per tick
  and the generation check that retires a stale snapshot's probers;
- `probe_lane` — the five skip guards (no key, `path_base` lane, OAuth token not yet minted), the
  request build (`probe_body` + `upstream_path_for_stream` + `sign_and_wire_path` + the lane's auth
  headers + CT/UA/Accept in the engine's exact insertion order), the send on the owned egress client
  under one deadline, the capped error-body read, the classification through
  `protocol_error → normalize_raw_error → classify`, and the four dispositions written into the
  lane store (`recover_lane`, `record_probe_success_all_cells`, `record_hard_down_all_cells`,
  `record_probe_failure_all_cells`).

It is composed once: `crates/busbar/src/main.rs:1437`, `busbar_llm::spawn_probers(&boot_host)`.

## 2. The four rows that block the move

**ROW 1 — the contract's probe face is not on this base.** `Plane::probe_request` does not exist at
`28b0fbe46`; the only `probe_request` in the tree is `busbar-llm-codec`'s per-writer JSON seam
(`proto_codec.rs:450` and the six writers). The face is PROBE-1's, parked at `ed537fe50` on
`origin/keep-health-probe-seam` (+5 declared surface, ARCHITECTURE §1.1 amended in the same commit,
probe-wire golden recorded 4/4 in `crates/busbar-plane-llm/tests/probe_wire.rs`). PROBE-1's base
`3f45e7e41` is an ancestor of this base, so the face is re-pickable — but MOVE 4 stacks on it and
cannot claim to add it.

**ROW 2 — the unit's grant cannot hold the loop.** `crates/busbar-unit-breaker/Cargo.toml` depends on
`busbar-caps` and `busbar-contract` and nothing else, and says so in its own header ("NO WORKSPACE
DEPENDENCIES BEYOND busbar-caps … no `axum`/`httpdate` … no `tracing`"). `health.rs` names `tokio`
(spawn, `interval_at`, `timeout_at`, `Instant`), `axum::http`, `http::Uri`/`HeaderMap`,
`hyper::body::Incoming`, `http_body_util`, `bytes`, `busbar_substrate::{breaker, plane_host,
store, proto, handlers}`, `busbar_api`, and seven items of `crate::engine`. Of the 535 lines, the
part expressible inside that grant is the pure schedule — roughly 120 lines (`ProbeSchedule`,
`advance_owned_deadline`, `elapsed_ms`, the first-deadline arithmetic and the mode decision), in the
same shape `cell.rs` already takes a supplied `now` rather than reading a clock. The other ~415 are
written in vocabulary a unit may not name.

**ROW 3 — the unit's book is not the served llm book.** `busbar_unit_breaker` is named in production
by exactly one file, `crates/busbar/src/root/adapters.rs` (`BreakerAdapter` over
`BreakerUnit<NoopJournal, DiagnosticsSink>`), and that adapter is read on the served path by the MCP
and A2A unit views only (`root/units_mcp.rs:688`, `root/units_a2a.rs:869`). The llm money path admits
and records through the substrate's lane store instead — `engine/select.rs:392` (`try_admit`),
`:577` (`ready_in`), `:581`/`:590` (`select_weighted_in`), `engine/pipeline.rs:1306` — which is the
same book `probe_lane` writes its four outcomes into. A schedule moved onto the unit therefore drives
cells that no llm request reads, and the deleted loop takes the served book's only prober with it.
This is PROBE-1's held-unit-half reason, re-measured and unchanged at this tip.

**ROW 4 — there is no legal edge to split the line across.** `kind-isolation:deps` scores every
dependency edge at the exact count its ledger row states, and an unlisted legacy→unit edge is RED by
construction — the gate's own selftest pins that case by name
(`xtask/src/gates/kind_isolation.rs:6842`, `busbar-llm` → `busbar-unit-audit`). The transitional
table in `qa/kind-isolation.toml` names `busbar-core → busbar-unit-*`, `busbar-core →
busbar-plane-admin` and `busbar-voice → busbar-plane-voice`, and no row for `busbar-llm`. So
`busbar-llm` cannot call a schedule that has moved to the unit, and adding the row would be widening
an exemption rather than applying one. The symmetric escape is closed too: the root may not grow the
reach that would let it drive the loop itself, because `legacy-reach:busbar_llm` only ever shrinks
(31 production reaches today), so a root-side prober that read llm lanes would red that row.

Consequences: the move is all-or-nothing, and all-or-nothing needs the served llm route step to be
served by `busbar-unit-breaker` (+ `busbar-unit-egress`) first. That is ROUTE-1/L1b's line, not this
one.

## 3. Headroom, and what a landable MOVE 4 must declare

`loc-ceilings:unit-total` measures **14,476 of a 14,476 ceiling — zero slack** (14 unit crates;
measured by pinning the ceiling low and reading the row's own detail). `union_ceiling` is 23,335 and
`caps_contract_ceiling` 3,480, both exact. So when MOVE 4 does land, its unit-side rise is a declared
MOVE by identity — the unit's rise equal to the lines `busbar-llm` loses, both numbers base→tip in
the same commit — and it must carry the union rise with it. There is no room to absorb it silently.

## 4. The recorded bytes MOVE 4 must not move

Measured against `testing/shadow-oracle/cells.json` (2,318 cells):

- `route.failover|*` (16) and `cooldown|trip-then-serve` (1) exercise the breaker's trip/cooldown
  cycle with **no `health:` block anywhere** — probing is `none` by default, and `cooldown-trip.sh`
  drives the passive path. Their bytes do not depend on the prober, which is why deleting it would
  come back green while the capability went dark: the golden cannot see this, exactly as §1.2 of the
  switch-over note says of the profiler.
- `boot.refusal|BOOT-089a|validate` and `|BOOT-089b|validate` pin the exact stderr of
  `health.default_probe_interval_secs must be >= 1` and `health.default_probe_timeout_secs must be
  >= 1`. Those two keys are the process-wide fallbacks the prober reads through the host seams. A
  MOVE 4 that re-homes the schedule must keep both keys and both messages byte-identical, or these
  two recorded cells move.
- The probe WIRE itself is recorded on PROBE-1's branch and nowhere on this base: the six bodies and
  six targets frozen as literals in `crates/busbar-plane-llm/tests/probe_wire.rs`. MOVE 4 is proven
  against that file, which is another reason it stacks on `ed537fe50`.

## 5. The order this makes

1. **PROBE-1** (`ed537fe50`) lands — the contract's probe face and the frozen probe wire.
2. **ROUTE-1 / L1b** lands — the served route step admits and records through `busbar-unit-breaker`,
   with the legacy `LaneRuntime`'s exact behaviour (nanos jitter seed, the passthrough 401/403
   exemption, the permit gate), so that ONE book answers for llm as it already does for mcp and a2a.
3. **MOVE 4** then becomes the move it was specified as, in one commit: the schedule and the health
   state onto `busbar-unit-breaker` (declared rise = `busbar-llm`'s fall, unit-total and union
   declared with §1.1), the request shape and answer reading in `busbar-plane-llm` behind
   `Plane::probe_request`, the tick-origin driver in the root over every plane rather than one, and
   `engine/health.rs` + `spawn_probers` deleted — with the red-first cell "a probe scheduled by the
   breaker unit reaches the plane's probe face and the breaker state moves" going green on the one
   book both halves now share.

Until step 2, MOVE 4's −535 is not available, and any commit that claimed it would be trading a live
capability for a number.
