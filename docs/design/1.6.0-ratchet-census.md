## What this document is, and what it is not

This is a **census, not a repair**. Nothing here was silenced. No ceiling was raised, no row was
struck, no exception was added. Where a number is red, it is recorded red. Where a row is dead
weight, it is named and left in place for its owner.

`qa/*.toml` is the largest reservoir of recorded-but-uncounted work in the tree. Every ratchet row,
every `known_missing_*`, every grandfather entry and every ceiling held red on purpose is a piece of
debt somebody wrote down and nobody has since totalled. This totals them.

### The four questions asked of every entry

1. **What debt does it hold** — the rule it excuses and the count it pins.
2. **Is the subject ALIVE?** A row naming a deleted crate or a moved path excuses nothing and hides
   nothing.
3. **Does it suppress anything TODAY?** The strong test. A row can pass a path-existence check and
   still be vacuous — the file exists but no longer holds the thing the row excuses.
4. **What would closing it take**, and is it blocked on a ruling.

## R.2 The bucket rule, stated before it is applied

A bucket assignment that is not mechanical is an opinion. These are the tests, and every row below
was decided by one of them:

- **SATISFIED** — the subject is alive, the gate row **passes today**, and the row carries **no
  drain**: its own text says there is nothing to pay down (an architecture-granted edge, a ratified
  dependency, a rule's own permitted door). It is a pin, not a debt.
- **OWED** — the subject is alive and the row **holds a live finding open**: remove the row and a
  gate goes red on something that is in the tree today. One line says what must change.
- **VACUOUS** — **the row cannot fail.** Its crate is deleted, its path is gone, its glob matches
  nothing, its kind has no member, its symbol is absent from the path it confines, or its ceiling
  sits above any value the tree can produce. **Zero is the passing answer to every ban.**
