# busbar 1.6.0 — Plugin ABI design review (fresh eyes)

Status: REVIEW + RECOMMENDATION. Modifies no ABI code. Written after the crate layout locked
(DECISIONS #33–#40). Method: (1) read the current design and its earned rationale; (2) design a
blank-sheet ABI without reference to the current one; (3) compare head-to-head; (4) two independent
red teams (opus, sonnet) attacked BOTH designs against real code; (5) adjudication.

**VERDICT: HYBRID.** The current design's *shape* — one shared FFI spine, per-kind faces, two lanes
chosen by heat — is the right shape and is the same shape a blank sheet produces today. Do not swap.
But the shape is *declared*, not *built*: the build graph contradicts "one contract, one loading
path" in three independent ways, the hot-path witnesses are source greps over a stub, and two
security holes (a forgeable `KernelSeal`, a bare `*mut c_void` host context) sit on the seam. Eight
fold-ins are listed in §6, ordered by value/risk; the first five are cheap and belong in 1.6.0; the
last three are the real content of any future redesign and must NOT be attempted mid-flight.

---

## 1. The question

Now that the crate layout is locked: ONE ABI for all plugin kinds, ONE per kind, or a shared spine
with divergent faces? Is the HOT/COLD split right? Is it worth swapping to something better, or is
the current design already excellent? Owner's prior: "v1 will have divergent ABIs for Store and
Plane, acceptable if EARNED not accidental; likely a shared spine with divergent faces."

## 2. The current design, and why (cited)

### 2.1 What the law says

- **#30 (kind→lane by heat):** two lanes on one seam. HOT = `#[repr(C)]` POD by pointer, zero-alloc,
  zero per-token plugin→host crossings, <1µs (LOCKED §2/§8) — for plane and transport, the only kinds
  inside the per-token loop, both new in 1.6.0 so no 1.5.5 byte constraint. COLD = serde-JSON per
  call — for store/secret/auth/hook/export, all JSON in 1.5.5 by deliberate design ("a serialize per
  call never touches request latency"), each firing per-request-once or write-behind, never per
  token; keeping them JSON is REQUIRED for byte-identity. Gate named: `kind-abi-lane`.
- **#38:** ONE contract crate `busbar-contract` holds all 7 kinds' traits + both lanes' wire types +
  neutral vocab; `busbar-contract-transport` folds in; sdk/loader depend on contract.
- **#33/#35:** plugin infra = `busbar-plugin-sdk` (author side: spec + FFI + `export_<kind>_plugin!`
  + testkit feature) + `busbar-plugin-loader` (host side: open/verify/call + sign + pack).
- **#40:** plugins are untrusted third parties. Three walls: dep-wall (closure = `busbar-contract`
  only), capability-ABI (privileged resources cross only as opaque `repr(C)` handles with no byte
  accessor), FFI vtable (host callbacks handed at init are the only way back). Posture A = native +
  signing; WASM tabled.
- **LOCKED §1 (forcing):** dynamic load crosses `.so`; Rust `dyn`/`Arc`/enums can't; one code path ⇒
  static uses the same `repr(C)` ABI, "dogfooding the host-vtable statically and dynamically
  identically". §2: POD-fast tier (args by pointer, results by value or into `&mut MaybeUninit<Out>`
  written inside `catch_unwind`; no `Vec` return, no malloc, no serde) + bytes tier (owned bytes for
  config/bodies). Budget ~10 POD calls/req × 2–5ns ⇒ ~100× under 1µs; "PROVEN by a step-0 perf
  spike before full build".
- **Taxonomy v5:** carrier axis (req/resp, response-stream, duplex), `WorkItem` reserved-shape with
  kind-tagged inbound/emit handles, three scopes (dispatch/session/durable), 2-tier egress, neutral
  capability set, neutrality grep. 17 adversarial passes ratified this spine.

What the 17 passes settled and this review does NOT reopen: `repr(C)` + sized/append-only +
major-airlock versioning; the existence of two tiers; opaque plane state; `DispatchScope` reclaim on
future drop; `WorkItem` reserved shape; credential-REF egress; no `secret_resolve` on the vtable.

### 2.2 What is actually built (this is where the review earns its keep)

There are not two lanes on one seam. There are **two seams in two type universes**, plus a third
plane representation:

| Seam | Where | Nature | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trait contract** | `crates/busbar-contract/src/` | `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` Rust traits with `'u` lifetimes, `&dyn ConfigView/SessionView/TransportView/Arena` in `Ctx<'u>`, `PlaneSessionState = Box<dyn Any + Send>`, `Ingress/Progress` enums carrying `Box<UnitDraft>/Box<Response>/Box<Facts>`, async `Transport` with `Pin<Box<dyn Future>>` per call | LIVE: 6 plane crates impl `Plane`, 8 transport crates impl `Transport`; kernel drives `decode_ingress` (`crates/busbar/src/root/units_mcp.rs:220`, `busbar-unit-egress/src/attempt.rs`) |
| **FFI COLD lane** | `crates/busbar-plugin/src/cold/` | airlock preamble (`BUSPLANE`, major 1 / minor 20); 6 kind-neutral `extern "C"` symbols (`busbar_abi/plugin_kind/open/call/free/close`), JSON bytes, plugin-alloc/plugin-free, `catch_unwind` at one choke point (`plugin-sdk/src/boundary.rs`) and again host-side | LIVE for 5 kinds (auth-static, secret-example, hook-test, export-example, store-example) |
| **FFI HOT lane** | `crates/busbar-plugin/src/hot/` | `PlaneHostVtable` (~25 `Option<extern "C-unwind" fn>` slots), `PlaneDecl`, `WorkItem`, `HostCtx = *mut c_void` | STUB: "every impl is a stub (`unimplemented!()`) … ADDITIVE and UNUSED — nothing in the engine calls it yet" (`hot/mod.rs:11-13`) |
| **Legacy `PLANE_DECL`** | `busbar-{mcp,llm,a2a,voice}/src/lib.rs` | the shipping 1.5.x plane registration | LIVE on the money path |

Facts that decide the review:

1. **The FFI seam does not speak the contract.** `crates/busbar-plugin/Cargo.toml` depends on
   `busbar-api`, not `busbar-contract`; so do `plugin-sdk` and `plugin-loader`. The only live
   dropped-in loading path speaks `busbar_api::Store` (the 1.5.5 JSON `StoreRequest` enum); the
   contract's `Store` (`kinds.rs:313-404`, 22 methods) has zero out-of-contract implementors. #38's
   "one contract crate" is prose; the build graph is two.
2. **Transport is not a dropped-in kind, and the contract says so.** `transport.rs:4-7`:
   "Transports are in-tree only and never dynamically loaded: they are inside the trusted computing
   base." The trait is async, returns `FrameStream`/`RawStream`, and `adopt` takes `&dyn Transport`.
   None of it crosses a cdylib under any C ABI. #3/#11/#39 require both-ways for all 7 kinds.
3. **The in-process host seam cannot lower to the vtable.** Planes hold `Arc<dyn EngineHost>`
   (`busbar-substrate/src/plane_host/mod.rs:1569`), 13 sub-traits with 10 `async fn`; the module doc
   explains `HostCtx` is `!Send` and frame-scoped, so the async seam exists *instead of* the vtable.
   LOCKED §1's "dogfood statically and dynamically identically" is unsatisfied as built.
4. **The perf/alloc witnesses do not measure anything.** `busbar-core/benches/plane_host_vtable_
   {perf,alloc}.rs` assert <1000ns p50/p99, 0 allocs, per-token host calls == 0 — over a vtable with
   one populated slot (`clock_now`) and "NO production rider". `xtask/src/gates/hot_path_perf.rs`
   is a substring grep for `"p50_delta_nanos"`/`"assert!"` in the bench source; `cargo bench` is
   never run. No number has ever been recorded. The step-0 perf spike LOCKED §7 gated the effort on
   has, in effect, not happened for a real rider.
5. **The `kind-abi-lane` gate does not exist** anywhere outside `DECISIONS.md` prose.
6. **The live plane path allocates per frame.** `Ingress::Frame{facts: Box<Facts>}` /
   `Progress::{Frame,Terminal}{r: Box<Response>}` are built per frame in 14+ sites across
   plane-{llm,mcp,streaming,voice}. For SSE, frame ≈ token. The boxing is justified in-line
   (`plane.rs:117-121`: a `Facts` is a >1 KiB fixed array; memcpy per frame is worse) — which is the
   tell that the *shape* is wrong, not the workaround.
7. **`KernelSeal` is forgeable and the contract proves it in a passing doctest.**
   `plugin.rs:189-203` implements `KernelSeal` on a foreign struct and mints a `VerifiedDestination`.
   Same constructor shape guards `TransportKeyHandle::issue` and `SecretOnce::mint`. The doc's own
   defence (`plugin.rs:172-173`: a loaded plugin is "signature-verified, operator-installed, trusted
   code, so that is not a line this system draws") is the direct negation of #40.
8. **`HostCtx` is a bare `*mut c_void`** (`hot/host.rs:42`), threaded through every slot, with no
   slot/generation/kind. A plane that stashes one past its frame has a use-after-free into engine
   state; nothing detects reuse. Harmless while the lane is a stub; a host-memory primitive the day
   the rider lands.
9. **Version constants are four-way incoherent.** `busbar_contract::STORE_ABI = AbiVersion(5)` has
   zero readers; the real store negotiation is `[STORE_ABI_FLOOR=2, cold::ABI_VERSION=4]` in
   `plugin-loader/src/registry.rs`, with `STORE_ABI_WITH_NEW_OPS = 5` in `store_adapter.rs`.
   `TRANSPORT_ABI = AbiVersion(1)` (trait-registry generation) and `cold::TRANSPORT_VERSION = 1`
   (symbol-set version) are unrelated axes at the same value.
10. **#40 is not in code.** `SecretValue::expose() -> &[u8]` is `pub` (`kinds.rs:431`, zero
    callers); `KeyMaterial.bytes` is a `pub` field. Already a HIGH in the codeaudit findings ledger.
11. **`ARENA_BYTES = 4 KiB` vs `MAX_CURSOR_BYTES = 64 KiB`** (`bounded.rs:63` vs `:30`). A legal
    64 KiB frame cannot be re-encoded through `encode_response -> ArenaBytes<'u>`; conformance tests
    in plane-mcp/plane-a2a encode the `ArenaBudget` refusal as expected. Documented, but a 16×
    ceiling mismatch on the relay path.
12. **A hung COLD plugin pins a blocking-pool thread.** `plugin-loader/src/hook.rs:94` concedes the
    deadline abandons the waiter, not the thread; `StoreRequest` carries no deadline field;
    `MAX_PLUGIN_RESPONSE_LEN = 256 MiB` per call.

### 2.3 The earned rationale, stated fairly

The heat discriminator (#30, "inside the per-token loop or not") is exactly right and is the only
principled way to partition seven kinds into two call conventions. Keeping the five cold kinds on
verbatim 1.5.5 JSON is not laziness; it is the oracle. The cold spine (`cold/mod.rs`) independently
arrived at "one kind-neutral symbol set, kind bound at load, JSON payload per kind" — a working,
shipped proof that spine + faces is viable and cheap. The trait contract's bounded/arena/seal
discipline (`BoundedVec<T,N>`, `ArenaBytes<'u>`, `Ctx<'u>` carrying exactly one resource, planes
returning locators never amounts) is a genuinely good plugin-facing surface. The airlock preamble +
sized/append-only structs + `write_out` into `MaybeUninit` under `catch_unwind` are the right FFI
primitives. None of this should be thrown away.

## 3. Blank-sheet design: SPINE + FACES, heat-encoded, trait-lowered

What I would build today for a thin engine, 7 kinds, compiled-in-or-dropped-in, per-token hot
path, native Rust, untrusted plugins.

- **S1 — ONE spine for every kind.** Airlock preamble (magic/major/minor); a fixed symbol set
  (`abi`, `kind`, `open`, `call`, `free`, `close`, plus one per-heat entry symbol for a POD decl);
  opaque instance handle; `extern "C-unwind"` everywhere a plugin is called, `catch_unwind` at one
  host choke point; every shared struct sized + append-only with a leading `size:u32, version:u16`;
  a host-callback vtable handed at `open` as the ONLY way back; mandatory signing; manifest declares
  kind + face-version.
- **S2 — FACES per kind, ENCODED by heat.** COLD faces = JSON bytes per call, kept verbatim (oracle).
  HOT face (plane) = `repr(C)` by pointer; results written into a caller-provided out-slot in the
  per-unit arena (no heap); crossing granularity is per FRAME host→plane, and zero plane→host
  callbacks per token. Cardinality of encodings is 2; cardinality of faces is 7.
- **S3 — the Rust traits are the author-facing contract; the SDK macro is the bijection.**
  `export_<kind>_plugin!` lowers a trait impl to its face; the compiled-in path fills the SAME
  vtable statically from the same impl. This forces the trait shapes to be *lowerable*: opaque
  state = `*mut c_void + drop fn` (not `Box<dyn Any>` — `TypeId` is not stable across separately
  compiled objects); no `Box<T>` returns (arena out-params); `&dyn *View` become snapshot PODs or
  handle + host-callback getters; `'u` borrows lower to `(ptr,len)` valid for the call.
- **S4 — versioning.** Spine major/minor + ONE per-face version constant, declared in exactly one
  place and read by the loader's `supported_abi(kind)` range; append-only within a major; every
  shared enum `#[repr(u8)]` with a reserved tail.
- **S5 — capability handles.** Every privileged resource is `#[repr(C)] Handle{slot:u64,
  generation:u32, kind:u8}` in a host-side table, usable only through host callbacks; the host
  context itself carries a generation. No raw secret bytes on any non-secret kind's surface.
- **S6 — transport.** An async trait with trait-object peers cannot cross a cdylib. Either declare
  transport a compiled-in TCB kind (documentation catching up to `transport.rs:5-7`) or design a
  readiness/poll C ABI — new work, not for a mid-flight release.
- **S7 — zero-copy.** Frames stay in the connection slab; planes get `&Frame`; relay bytes borrow.
  The arena must be sized for the frames it is asked to re-encode, or the API must be chunked.
- **S8 — FFI native (posture A).** The COLD faces are *nearly* WASM-portable (bytes in/out) but not
  by construction: the plugin-allocates/host-frees buffer and the log-sink `*mut c_void` are
  cross-module allocator/pointer contracts with no WASM analogue. Earn it later; don't claim it.

### 3.1 Head-to-head

| Axis | Current (as built) | Fresh | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spine | shared preamble + 6 symbols + `write_out` + `catch_unwind` — good | same | none |
| Faces | 5 COLD JSON faces (live), 1 HOT POD face (stub), transport = Rust trait | 5 COLD + 1 HOT + transport as TCB | fresh only makes the transport truth explicit |
| Lanes | 2 by heat, correct discriminator | 2 by heat | none |
| Type universe | FFI speaks `busbar-api`; traits in `busbar-contract`; never reconciled | one | **real gap**, multi-week |
| Loading path | trait (compiled-in) ≠ vtable (dropped-in); host seam async, un-lowerable | one, via lowerable traits | **real gap**, blocked on `EngineHost` sync/async split |
| Hot-path proof | grep of bench text over a stub | executed bench on a rider | cheap to fix the gate; rider is the keystone |
| Versioning | 4 incoherent constants | 1 per face | mechanical |
| Handles | `TransportKeyHandle` opaque; `SecretValue::expose` pub; `HostCtx` bare ptr; `KernelSeal` forgeable | generation-checked handles, sealed | **security**, cheap while unwired |
| Per-frame alloc | Box per frame | arena out-slot | shape change across 6 crates |
| Arena sizing | 4 KiB vs 64 KiB cursor | must be decided | unresolved in both |

The fresh design is the current design with its promises kept. It is not a different shape.

## 4. Adversarial findings

Two independent red teams (opus, sonnet), each attacking both designs against real code.

### 4.1 Where they agreed

- Bottom line **HYBRID**, both. SWAP is not survivable before 2026-10-08; KEEP ships unverified
  perf claims and (opus) two security holes.
- The hot-path witnesses are "theatre" (opus) / "prove nothing" (sonnet): grep gate, stub vtable,
  no recorded number, no `cargo bench` in CI.
- The `kind-abi-lane` gate is missing; #30's central invariant is enforced nowhere.
- Per-frame `Box` on the declared-hot path is the largest claim/reality mismatch on perf.
- Version constants have drifted; the airlock does not prevent it because the constants belong to
  different wires.
- The fresh design's S3 "bijection" is asserted, not designed (sonnet: "a rewrite disguised as
  SPINE + FACES"; opus: refuted outright — the host direction is async, and a macro cannot bridge
  `async fn` to `extern "C"`).
- S1 ("6 symbols, all 7 kinds") is inconsistent with S6 (transport can't); the plane already needs
  a 7th symbol (`busbar_plane_decl`).
- S7's arena question is raised and not answered by either design.
- Transport belongs in a third category — compiled-in TCB — not in the HOT lane.

### 4.2 Opus-only findings (verified by me before adoption)

- **A1 CRITICAL:** `busbar-plugin`/`plugin-sdk`/`plugin-loader` depend on `busbar-api`, never on
  `busbar-contract` (verified in all three `Cargo.toml`s). Two disjoint type universes.
- **A2 CRITICAL:** three plane representations coexist (contract trait, hot `PlaneDecl` stub,
  legacy `PLANE_DECL`), with a name collision on `PLANE_DECL`.
- **A3 CRITICAL:** `KernelSeal` forgery doctest (verified: `plugin.rs:189` is a plain ``` ``` ```
  block, not `compile_fail`).
- **A5 HIGH:** `EngineHost` = 13 async sub-traits; structurally un-lowerable.
- **A6 HIGH:** `HostCtx = *mut c_void` (verified `hot/host.rs:42`), no generation.
- **A8 HIGH:** COLD blocking pool pinning; `StoreRequest` has no deadline.
- **B3 HIGH:** the fresh design's "WASM-portable by construction" claim is false today (allocator
  contract + log-sink pointer). Accepted; folded into S8 above.
- **B4/B5:** sealing `expose()` behind a forgeable `KernelSeal` is not sealing; signing proves
  provenance, not behaviour, so posture A over a shared address space with A3+A6 is not isolation.
  Accepted as the honest ceiling; #40 already states channel-2 is walled only by a sandbox, but A3
  means channel-1 is not yet walled either.

### 4.3 Sonnet-only emphasis

- The cold spine (`cold/mod.rs`) is itself the proof that spine + faces works; plane's mistake was
  getting a *second* spine rather than a POD face on the same discovery pipeline.
- "One per kind is what happens by default if nobody unifies plane's two seams — the repo is
  trending there by neglect, not by decision."
- Migration plan for the 13-subtrait `EngineHost` into one C vtable is unscoped in the fresh design.

### 4.4 Where they disagreed

**Is the Store-vs-Plane divergence EARNED or ACCIDENTAL?** Sonnet: accidental — plane's "hot"
implementation is an unaudited, box-allocating trait path wearing a label it hasn't earned. Opus:
earned on the *call convention* (JSON-per-request vs POD-per-frame is a real µs-vs-ns decision,
argued correctly in `cold/mod.rs:18-21`), accidental on the *type universe, vocabulary and
versioning* (that store speaks `busbar_api`, plane speaks `busbar_contract`, and they carry four
incoherent constants follows from history, not heat).

## 5. Adjudication

Both red teams reached HYBRID independently, so there is no bottom-line tiebreak. On the one
substantive disagreement I rule with **opus, with sonnet's caveat attached**: the divergence is
earned in principle and accidental in fact. The owner's stated acceptance criterion was "acceptable
if EARNED not accidental"; the honest reading is that the *decision* to diverge was earned (#30's
discriminator is correct and the JSON/POD split follows from it) but the *artefact* that exists
today diverges for reasons heat does not explain: two crates, two vocabularies, four version
constants, and a hot lane that is a stub while the live path allocates per frame. The fix for the
accidental part is reconciliation, not redesign — which is what makes HYBRID rather than SWAP the
answer.

On the three-way question:

- **ONE ABI for all kinds:** wrong. A JSON `call` cannot carry a per-token POD frame under 1µs; a
  POD vtable cannot preserve 1.5.5 JSON byte-identity. Both red teams and the blank sheet agree.
- **ONE ABI per kind:** wrong. Seven airlocks, seven `catch_unwind`s, seven version schemes for two
  call conventions is duplication with no benefit — and sonnet is right that the tree is drifting
  toward it by neglect (plane's second spine).
- **Shared spine, divergent faces, two encodings by heat:** right. It is what the law says, what the
  cold lane already proves, and what a blank sheet produces. Face cardinality 7, encoding
  cardinality 2, spine cardinality 1.

On HOT/COLD: the axis and the discriminator are right; the cardinality is 2; but **transport is
mis-filed**. It is a compiled-in TCB kind (the contract says so; the trait cannot cross a cdylib),
not a HOT-lane plugin. The honest lane table is HOT = plane (1), COLD = 5, TCB = transport (1).

On the perf witness: the owner asked that the lane split be confirmed "with the <1µs perf witness +
alloc profile expectations rather than shape-analysis alone". It cannot be. The witness has never
run against a rider; the alloc profile of the live path is one `Box` per frame. The split survives
on the strength of the discriminator argument, not on measurement. That is acceptable for a design
review; it is not acceptable for a release note.

## 6. Verdict and fold-ins

**HYBRID.** Keep the shape. Do not swap. Fold in, ordered by value/risk; items 1–5 are cheap,
additive within `ABI_MAJOR = 1`, touch no money-path byte, and belong in 1.6.0. Items 6–8 are the
substantive content of the fresh design and are multi-week reconciliations; they need an owner
ruling on timing because 1.6.0 bans deferral language and this doc will not use it.

**Why not SWAP:** the fresh design's centrepiece (S3 bijection) is blocked on the `EngineHost`
async/sync split, its COLD half is blocked on the `busbar-api` type-universe fold against a never-
waived oracle, and neither can land in the three weeks to the cut. A swap would jeopardise the 1.6.0
cut for zero user-visible gain.

**Why not KEEP:** items 1 and 4 are shippable security holes on the seam #40 claims is walled, and
item 3 means no hot-path claim in the release is defensible. These are cheap and the cost of leaving
them rises sharply once the rider lands.

**Owner rulings needed** (this doc does not make them): (a) #40 vs `plugin.rs:172-173` — is a signed
cdylib trusted on channel-1 or not; (b) transport's honest classification (fold-in 2); (c) the timing
of fold-in 8 relative to the cut, given #15.
