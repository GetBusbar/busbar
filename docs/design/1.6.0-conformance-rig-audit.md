# 1.6.0 — CONFORMANCE RIG AUDIT

**Question asked:** a large share of this release's evidence is delegated to "the conformance rigs".
**Do they exist, do they run, and can they be shown failing?**

**Measured** 2026-09-22 on `consolidated/1.6.0` @ `45104e7f791b25e867737b38661cae46b1b6e7bf`.
This document **MEASURES ONLY**. Nothing was repaired. Every plant made to test a rig was reverted
and the revert verified byte-identical to the HEAD blob before any verdict here was written.

## EVIDENCE HOLES

Ranked by how much release evidence rests on each.

### H-1 — 1,380 oracle cells (59.5% of the corpus) defer to a rig. Half of the MCP half names a method the battery never mentions.

`testing/shadow-oracle/golden/1.5.5/ledger.tsv` is 2,318 rows: **916 PASS, 1,402 SKIP**.
Of the SKIPs, **1,380 carry the verbatim reason *"proven by its conformance rig, not recorded here"***
— 912 mcp + 468 a2a. That is **59.5% of the corpus**, not the 39% the mcp figure alone suggests;
**the a2a 468 make the identical claim and are subject to the identical audit.**

The 912 mcp cells span **35 distinct methods** × 2 transports (`stdio`, `streamable-http`).
Against that, the in-house battery at `testing/mcp-conformance/` names 21 method-shaped strings in
its entire source tree. Cross-referencing cell method against battery source:

```
TOTAL mcp SKIP cells: 912
cells whose method the battery NEVER names, anywhere in its source: 456
```

The 456 include **`initialize`** and **`ping`** — the two most basic methods in the protocol — plus
`logging/setLevel`, `roots/list`, `resources/subscribe`, `resources/unsubscribe`, `completion/complete`,
all three `tasks/*`, `GET /mcp (open SSE stream)`, `DELETE /mcp (terminate session)`, and six
`notifications/*`.

And "named by the battery" is a **deliberately generous bar** — it counts a string appearing anywhere
in the battery source, including comments and the negative-control's deliberate-nonsense list. The
number of methods the battery **actually sends** is smaller still, and is stated by the plane's own
conformance suite at `crates/busbar-plane-mcp/tests/conformance.rs:196-198`:

> *"The battery's suites send these four and no others, so this is the narrowest statement that
> covers what a run actually exercises."* — `server/discover`, `tools/list`, `tools/call`,
> `subscriptions/listen`.

**So: 912 cells were skipped in favour of a rig whose suites send four methods.**

### H-2 — Two of the five MUST planes have zero oracle cells AND zero rig. Nothing covers them.

Measured from the ledger's plane field: the only plane values present are `a2a`, `llm`, `mcp`,
`billing`, `boot.refusal`, `http.crosscut`. `BUSBAR-1.6.0.md:1439` states it plainly and correctly:

> *"**zero streaming and zero decision cells exist at any status.**"*

The same sentence concludes *"the witness belongs in the rigs."* For these two planes **there is no
rig to belong to**: no `testing/streaming-conformance/`, no `testing/decision-conformance/`, no
jev rig. The registry's `jev` suite is `status: "not-run"`. So for streaming and decision the oracle
defers to the rigs and the rigs do not exist — **a gap rendering as neither a failure nor a gap.**

### H-3 — The ten GREEN conformance verdicts are hand-committed fixtures, not CI output, and they are stale.

All ten `conformance/verdicts/*.json` marked `status:"pass"` carry:

- `run_id` / `evidence` of `https://github.com/GetBusbar/busbar/actions/runs/**1201**` … `**1210**`
  — **sequential four-digit integers**. A real GitHub Actions run id is a ~10-digit number.
- `"generated_at": "2026-09-19T14:05:00Z"` on **all ten**, identical to the second.
- `"commit": "67ee7910305068c2c86ccd1811d4bd189973b45b"` on all ten.

**The timestamps are impossible.** Commit `67ee791` is authored `2026-09-20T20:36:19-07:00`
(= `2026-09-21T03:36Z`). Every verdict claims to have been generated **~37 hours before the commit
it certifies existed.**

The mechanism is on the record. `2744fc6aa` — *"W7.b: re-record the 4 wired planes' conformance
verdicts on HEAD"* — touched 11 files for `21 insertions(+), 21 deletions(-)`: **one changed line per
verdict**, the `commit` field. No suite was re-run; `run_id` and `generated_at` were left untouched.
"Re-record" was a **SHA edit**, and it is what turned ten stale rows green.

Independently: **no workflow anywhere uploads a `conformance-verdict-*` artifact.**
`grep -rn "conformance-verdict" .github/workflows/*.yml` → zero hits. All 17 registry suites have no
producer. `conformance/README.md:14-22` still says the generator that would download real artifacts,
`cargo xtask conformance-manifest --write`, is *"NOT YET BUILT"*.
