## 0. THE METHOD — count-in = count-out, per source

For every place work can be recorded, three numbers:

| | meaning |
|---|---|
| **COUNT IN** | what the source mechanically contains, extracted by a command printed in this file |
| **COUNT OUT** | how many of those reached **the master list** (defined in §1) as a row somebody owns |
| **DELTA** | `IN − OUT`. A non-zero delta is either an extraction bug or a dropped item. Finding those is the entire point. |

**A delta is not a failure to be hidden. An unexplained delta is.** Every non-zero delta below is
either reconciled in prose or named as unreconciled. Nothing is rounded, nothing is asserted from
memory, and every number carries the command that produces it.

## A.3 WHY H5's FIXTURES ARE OUTCOME (2) AND NOT A DODGE

This is the one ruling that most resembles the evasion this exercise exists to catch, so it gets its
proof rather than its assertion.

`mod stub` / `PlaneHostVtable::STUB` / `PlaneDecl::STUB` are a **compile-surface proof**: each fn
carries the exact fn-pointer signature of its vtable slot, so the module type-checks that every
`extern "C-unwind"` slot has a real, well-typed signature, and a STUB slot panics *because nothing is
meant to dispatch through it*. The test that it is a fixture and not a postponement is **who calls
it**, and that is measurable:

```sh
grep -rn 'PlaneHostVtable::STUB\|PlaneDecl::STUB' crates/ | grep -v '^crates/busbar-plugin/src/hot/\(host\|decl\).rs:'
# -> crates/busbar-plugin/src/hot/tests/host_tests.rs:77,128,138,145,157,222
#    crates/busbar-plugin/src/hot/tests/decl_tests.rs:29
```

**Seven call sites, all of them the tests that check its types.** A fixture whose only callers are
its own type-checks is finished, and no future release will "fix" it — it gets deleted when the thing
it stands in for arrives.

**What the H5 wording was hiding is a different row, and that one IS 1.6.0.** `BUSBAR-1.6.0.md`
§11a makes *"`PlaneHost` (+ cost carrier) has REAL production riders (dogfooded, step 2) — no
'0-caller ABI'"* a **CUT GATE** (§11, *"rerun … as CUT GATES … and GATE the 1.6.0 cut"*). And the gap
is **not** the one the phrase suggests:

```sh
sed -n '36,140p' crates/busbar-kernel/src/plane_host/vtable.rs | grep -cE '^\s+[a-z_]+: Some\('
# -> 44          (44 of 44 slots wired to real impls; 13,054 lines under plane_host/)
grep -rln 'PlaneHostVtable' crates/busbar-plane-* crates/busbar-{llm,mcp,a2a,voice} --include='*.rs'
# -> (empty)     ZERO plane crates cross the C-ABI vtable
```

**The host is built; nothing dogfoods it.** All four installed planes ride the neutral Rust seam
(`EngineHost`/`MeteringHost`); the C-ABI vtable is crossed only host-to-itself inside the kernel, and
`plane_host/mod.rs:190` says so: *"ADDITIVE and currently unridden: no plane dispatch is wired yet."*
That is **H6**, and **nothing in CI reds on it** — `plane-abi-neutrality`'s six rows are neutrality
and presence checks, not rider counts, so §11a is today a prose gate adjudicated by a human rerun.

**The two honest outcomes are: land a rider, or have the owner amend §11a's bullet.** "Ship and call
§11a satisfied" is not one of them.

## A.5 PART C — THE DETECTOR, AND WHAT IT STILL CANNOT DO

`scripts/deferral-words.py`. Two layers, and the difference is the point.

**Layer 1 — named shapes, the enforcement set.** 22 regex families, case-insensitive, each derived
from a phrasing this tree actually used: `defer-family`, `out-of-scope`, `next-version`,
`extension-point`, `phase-n`, `wave-later`, `future-release`, `not-yet-done`, `negated-now`,
`undecided`, `for-now`, `partial-coverage`, `approximation`, `stub-admission`, `someone-elses-row`,
`denies-deferral`, and others.

**Layer 2 — two structural rules, the reading list.** No deferral vocabulary at all. **(A)** a
future-time signal paired with a work signal in one line; **(B)** a modal obligation paired with an
indefinite or absent agent. Noisy by construction, never a gate — its job is the phrasing nobody
enumerated.


**1 of 5. I am reporting that rather than fixing it, and the refusal is deliberate.** Two earlier
rounds *were* used to widen the shape set — disclosed in the file, and both widenings were new
SHAPE CLASSES (`negated-now`; `partial-coverage`; `approximation`; `stub-admission`;
`someone-elses-row`; Layer 2's obligation rule), never the words of the phrase that exposed them.
Round 3 is what the detector does on prose it has not seen. **1 in 5.**

So, plainly: **Layer 1 is a shape set, not a complete one, and no amount of widening will close
this.** "More plumbing than this ticket bought" and "documented and unenforced" are deferrals with no
deferral vocabulary, no future tense, and no unowned modal. A text detector cannot reach them, and
tuning it against each new example just rebuilds the spelling list one word at a time — which is the
exact failure the four-literal grep already demonstrated.

**The correct conclusion is the one §9.5 reached and this appendix confirms with a number: the ban
cannot be enforced by a detector alone.** The detector's real job is to make the 665 candidate lines
*findable* so a human can read them, and to make the 93→665 widening permanent so the cheapest
evasion — reordering two words — stops working. The reading is still the gate. `--layer=2` and
`--summary` exist for exactly that, and `--selftest` prints the 1-in-5 every time it is run, so the
limitation is stated by the tool rather than remembered about it.
