## 3. Why not a `busbar-unit-observability`, and why the Tick is real

CORE-HOMES §2.4's reasoning holds and is not re-argued: telemetry emission is not a step (no
`Admit`, no token, no verdict), it fires from six places on the request path, and no `busbar-unit-*`
crate holds observability-shaped code. Making a unit for the EMIT spends `unit_total_ceiling` on
plumbing and puts a mandatory crate between the hot path and an atomic.

The DRAIN is the opposite shape and that is why exactly one thing here becomes a Tick. It is
periodic, it is not on the request path, it has a cadence derived from operator config, and it has
a correctness obligation on shutdown (the final drain) that the current detached thread cannot
meet. Everything else in the family is pull-driven off the scrape
(`busbar-substrate/src/telemetry.rs:507-515` from `metrics::render()`).
