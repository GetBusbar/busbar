# 1.6.0 — G6: the concrete-IR move (build plan)

Status: **DESIGN, pre-code.** This is the freeze-gating refactor (FB-5). It is multi-session and
cycle-sensitive; this doc is the plan that must be right before the first type moves. Companion to
`1.6.0-protocol-plugin-abi.md` (the ABI) and `1.6.0-abi-solidification.md` (gate G6).

Authority: the owner's acceptance test governs — *core + llm plugin ≡ 1.5.4, byte-identical*. Every
slice below is byte-identity-preserving or it does not land.

---

## 0. Correcting the mental model (what G6 actually is)

Three beliefs from earlier notes were wrong or imprecise. The reconnaissance (2026-08-19) settles them:

1. **The dialect codecs have ALREADY moved.** All six dialects
   (`anthropic/ bedrock/ cohere/ gemini/ openai_chat/ openai_responses/`) live in `crates/busbar-llm/`
   today, each with the five-file contract (`mod/reader/writer/handler/tests`). `busbar-llm/src/lib.rs`
   exports `pub static DECLS` (the ordered `ProtocolDecl` slice). **The move is not the codecs.**

2. **What remains in core is the CONCRETE IR ITSELF** — `crates/busbar-core/src/ir/` — plus the
   in-core *translation orchestration* (`proxy/`, `proto/`) that still names those concrete types.

3. **The neutral trait is `IrFacts`** (`ir/facts.rs:309`), not `PlaneFacts`. `PlaneFacts`/`plane/` is
   the separate protocol-blind plane subsystem; do not conflate them. `IrFacts` + its projection
   vocabulary (`Slot`, `ContentItem<'a>`, `Shape`) is the neutral surface the shared pipeline reads.

4. **The "2599 refs" are the UPWARD dependency.** They are `busbar-llm/src/` → `busbar_core::ir::`.
   The plugin reaches *up* into core for the concrete IR that logically belongs beside it. G6 relocates
   the concrete IR *down* so those 2599 refs become local `crate::ir::` — that is the payoff.

**G6 in one sentence:** move the concrete LLM IR (and the LLM-specific translation/billing that reads
it) out of `busbar-core` into `busbar-llm`, leaving core with the neutral router + the `IrFacts` trait
+ the two protocol-neutral operations, so that removing the LLM plugin leaves a core that still routes
MCP and A2A.

---

## 1. The cycle — why "move a leaf first" is UNSAFE

The tempting first slice ("`ir::rerank` has zero in-core production consumers, move it") **breaks the
build**. Proof, from code:

- `ir/variant.rs:39` — `pub enum IrReq` wraps **every** concrete leaf by value:
  `Chat(IrRequest)`, `Embeddings(EmbeddingsReq)`, `Moderation(ModerationReq)`, `Image(ImageReq)`,
  `Transcription(TranscriptionReq)`, `Speech(SpeechReq)`, `Rerank(rerank::RerankReq)`, plus the two
  neutral operations `Invoke(InvokeReq)`, `Subscribe(SubscribeReq)`.
- `IrReq` lives in **core**. `busbar-llm` **depends on `busbar-core`** (`busbar-llm/Cargo.toml`:
  `busbar-core = { path = "../busbar-core" }`); core does **not** depend on busbar-llm.

Therefore: moving `RerankReq` into `busbar-llm` while `IrReq::Rerank(rerank::RerankReq)` stays in core
makes **core name a busbar-llm type → a dependency cycle**. Cargo rejects it. The enum hub is the
gravity well: no concrete leaf can leave core until the hub itself is resolved.

**Consequence:** G6 is a *coordinated cutover*, not a drip of independent leaf moves. The safe unit of
work before the cutover is **dependency inversion inside core** — shrinking the set of core sites that
name concrete IR — not relocation.

---

## 2. The three kinds of core→concrete-IR reference

The 480 in-core `crate::ir::` refs (778 by type name) are not one problem. They are three, and each has
a different disposition. Classifying every site into one of these buckets IS the bulk of G6 execution.

**(A) Neutral-pipeline, ALREADY inverted — nothing to do.** The gate/hook/screening path consumes the
IR only through `IrFacts` (`subject.facts.content()`, `.shape()`, `.verb()`). These sites never name a
concrete type; they are the proof the seam works and the template for the rest.

**(B) LLM translation/billing — RELOCATES to busbar-llm (does NOT invert).** These sites genuinely need
the concrete representation because they translate provider wire ↔ IR. They are LLM-plugin logic that
was simply never moved out. Canonical example: `proxy/usage.rs` — `usage_from_tapped_value()`
(`:143`) switches on `"anthropic"` / `"bedrock"` / … to decode provider usage JSON into `IrUsage`;
`tier_tokens()` (`:235`) — already named in ABI §4.3.1 as busbar-llm-bound. This whole family
(`proxy/usage.rs`, the concrete-IR half of `proto/stream.rs`, `proto/mod.rs`'s translate paths, and
`proxy/wire.rs::translate_request_cross_protocol`) moves *with* the IR, invoked afterward by core
through the neutral `DECLS` registry rather than inline.

**(C) The enum hub + trait impl — SPLITS.** `variant.rs` mixes LLM operations (`Chat…Rerank`) with the
two neutral operations (`Invoke`, `Subscribe`) that the A2A/MCP planes use (`a2a/receive.rs:895`,
`mcp/method.rs:1327` — `ir::invoke::InvokeReq`). `impl IrFacts for IrRequest` (`facts.rs:339`) is the
seam that must follow `IrRequest` down. This bucket is the cutover core and needs an owner decision
(§4, Q-A/Q-B).

The disposition rule: **(A)** is done, **(B)** relocates, **(C)** is the designed cutover. G6 progress =
converting (B) sites to relocated + (C) resolved, until core names zero LLM-concrete IR types.

## 3. End state (what lands where)

**STAYS in busbar-core (the neutral router):**
- `trait IrFacts` + `Slot` + `ContentItem<'a>` + `Shape` + `project()` (`ir/facts.rs`) — the projection
  vocabulary every plane's facts implement.
- The two protocol-neutral operations `InvokeReq/Resp` (`ir/invoke.rs`) and `SubscribeReq/Resp`
  (`ir/subscribe.rs`) — used by the neutral A2A/MCP planes, so they cannot live in an LLM crate.
- The neutral router surface: `proto/registry.rs`, `proto/detect.rs`, protocol registration, and the
  engine that drives translation *through* `DECLS` without naming a concrete IR.
- `proto/openai_family.rs` — its `KIND_*`/`ERR_TYPE_*` bank is consumed by `proxy`/`admin`/`auth` in
  production; moving it inverts the seam. Explicitly stays (recon-confirmed).
- The session/lineage/cost/host primitives already landed.

**MOVES to busbar-llm (the LLM plugin):**
- The chat IR: `IrRequest`, `IrResponse`, `IrMessage`, `IrBlock`, `IrRole`, `IrTool`, `IrUsage`,
  `IrDelta`, `IrStreamEvent`, `IrStopReason`, `IrMediaKind`, `IrCitation`, reasoning/format/cache
  types, `StreamDecodeState` (`ir/mod.rs`).
- The LLM operation leaves: `image`, `audio`, `embeddings`, `rerank`, `moderation` (`ir/*.rs`).
- `impl IrFacts for IrRequest` (the impl follows the type; the trait stays).
- The (B) translation/billing: `proxy/usage.rs`, the concrete half of `proto/stream.rs`,
  `proxy/wire.rs` translate driver, `proto/mod.rs` translate paths.
- The LLM arms of `variant.rs` (see Q-A for whether the enum splits or genericizes).

**Cardinal invariant:** after G6, `grep -r 'IrRequest\|IrBlock\|IrUsage' crates/busbar-core/src`
returns **zero** production hits. That grep is the freeze witness for G6.

## 4. Open design questions for the owner (BLOCKING the cutover)

**Q-A — where does the `IrReq`/`IrResp` hub live, and how do the two neutral operations stay out of
busbar-llm?** Options:
  - **A1 (split the enum):** core keeps a neutral `enum Operation2 { Invoke, Subscribe }` (or folds
    Invoke/Subscribe onto the plane-facts path directly), busbar-llm owns `enum LlmReq { Chat…Rerank }`.
    Cleanest separation; costs the exhaustive-match-guarantee that today's single `IrReq` gives.
  - **A2 (genericize the hub):** `IrReq` becomes generic / trait-object over `IrFacts`, stays in core,
    holds no concrete type. Preserves one dispatch point; harder to keep byte-identical through the
    match sites.
  - **A3 (move the whole hub to busbar-llm, re-home Invoke/Subscribe first):** relocate `invoke`/
    `subscribe` onto the neutral plane path, then the entire `variant.rs` follows the LLM IR down.
  - *Recommendation to ratify: A3.* Invoke/Subscribe are already the odd arms (both `allow(dead_code)`,
    both protocol-surface not LLM-surface); re-homing them onto the neutral seam removes the mix, after
    which `variant.rs` is purely LLM and moves whole. Owner to confirm.

**Q-B — one shared `busbar-ir` leaf crate, or straight into busbar-llm?** A shared leaf both crates
depend on would break the cycle trivially — but it would leave **core depending on LLM-shaped types**,
defeating the neutral-router goal (removing the LLM plugin must remove the LLM IR). *Recommendation:
NO shared crate; the concrete IR lands inside busbar-llm.* Confirm this is the owner's intent (it is
the whole thesis, but it is the expensive path, so it must be explicit).

**Q-C — how does core's engine invoke translation after the move?** Today `proto`/`proxy` translate
inline. After the move, core must drive translation through the neutral `DECLS` registry / a host
vtable (the `PlaneHost`/`PlaneDecl` seam from the ABI doc), passing opaque bytes and receiving the
neutral `IrFacts` projection. This is the ABI's `repr(C)`-deferred, in-process-vtable ruling (D8)
applied to the IR boundary. Confirm the translation entrypoint is a `DECLS` method, not a core-side
concrete call.

**Q-D — cutover atomicity.** The concrete-IR cluster (mod.rs + variant.rs + leaves + the (B)
translation) is mutually referential; realistically it moves in **one commit** behind the dual-compile
net, not incrementally. Confirm the owner accepts one large green cutover commit (all witnesses green)
rather than many partial ones for this specific cluster.

## 5. Cycle-safe ordered sequence

Inversion and re-homing first (each byte-identical, independently landable), then one cutover.

1. **Re-home the neutral operations (Q-A/A3).** Move `Invoke`/`Subscribe` off the LLM `IrReq` hub onto
   the neutral plane-facts path used by A2A/MCP. Leaves `variant.rs` purely LLM. *Witness: a2a/mcp
   gate tests, invoke/subscribe tests.* Independently landable.
2. **Relocate the (B) translation/billing incrementally where a site can move without the types.** Any
   (B) function that can be expressed as "core calls a `DECLS` method" gets that indirection introduced
   NOW (still resolving to the in-core codecs via the `#[path]` dual-compile), so the call shape is the
   post-move shape before the types move. *Witness: usage/billing tests, translate goldens.*
3. **Introduce the translation entrypoint on `DECLS`/host vtable (Q-C).** Core stops calling concrete
   translate functions by path; it calls the registry. Behind the vtable, the impl is unchanged.
   *Witness: translate_parity_golden, roundtrip_fidelity, stream_translate — all green.*
4. **THE CUTOVER (Q-D).** Move the concrete IR cluster + `impl IrFacts for IrRequest` + `variant.rs` +
   the (B) bodies into busbar-llm in one commit. The 2599 busbar-llm refs become local; core's
   concrete-IR grep goes to zero. The dual-compile `#[path]` net (§6) keeps core's test binary
   compiling the real codecs+IR throughout. *Witness: the full witness set (§6), byte-for-byte.*
5. **Delete the dual-compile scaffolding** once core no longer needs the IR types in its own test
   build (or keep it as the differential harness — decide at step 4).

Only after step 4 is G6 "done" for the freeze gate. Steps 1–3 are safe to land unattended and one at a
time; step 4 is the owner-gated big commit.

---

## 6. The witness set (must be green at EVERY slice)

These pin the IR's observable behavior independent of where the types are declared:
- `proxy/tests/hook_ir_differential_tests.rs` — the sharpest: every fixture's `IrFacts` projection vs a
  pinned golden across all six wire formats (`the_surviving_projection_matches_its_pinned_golden`).
- `ir/tests/facts_tests.rs` — the projection properties over hand-built IR.
- `proto/tests/translate_parity_golden_tests.rs` + `proto/tests/golden/` corpus — cross-protocol
  translation goldens (`req_a2o_*.json`, `resp_o2a_*.json`).
- `proto/tests/roundtrip_fidelity_tests.rs`, `same_proto_fidelity_tests.rs` — ingress→IR→egress.
- `proto/tests/stream_translate_tests.rs`, `stream_fanout_tests.rs` — streaming translation.
- The matrix witnesses: `response_format_matrix_tests.rs`, `stop_reason_matrix_tests.rs`,
  `image_source_matrix_tests.rs`.

The dual-compile net that keeps them meaningful during the move: `proto/mod.rs` `#[path]`-includes the
six busbar-llm dialect sources into core's test/`test-support` build (`proto/mod.rs:1896–1929`), so the
real codecs compile under `crate::proto::{anthropic,…}` regardless of their crate home. This is what
lets a witness in core exercise a codec that lives in busbar-llm.

---

## 7. Effort shape

- Steps 1–3: several small byte-identical commits; landable unattended against the witness set.
- Step 4: one large commit, owner-gated (Q-A..Q-D answered), all witnesses green, the concrete-IR grep
  in core at zero.
- Risk concentrates entirely in step 4; steps 1–3 de-risk it by making the *call shapes* final before
  the *type homes* change. The dual-compile net means a regression shows up as a witness diff, not a
  silent behavior change.

**Next action when execution resumes:** answer Q-A..Q-D with the owner, then land step 1 (re-home
Invoke/Subscribe) as the first byte-identical slice.
