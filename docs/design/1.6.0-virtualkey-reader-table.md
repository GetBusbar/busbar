# MEASURE: every reader of `VirtualKey`, by what it reads

Owner ruling 13:0x (6): *the enforced key's ONE shape for 1.6.0 is `ResolvedKey` in
`busbar-unit-auth`; legacy `busbar_core::governance::VirtualKey` dies with core; the money-carrying
fields VirtualKey holds today move to the cost/ledger VIEWS.*

This file is the measurement that ruling is executed against. It is a census, not a plan: nothing
here proposes a change. What it establishes is which fields of the key are actually READ, by whom,
and under which of the six headings the ruling names — identity, scope, budget, rate card, expiry,
revocation.

## Where the type actually lives

`VirtualKey` is defined at `crates/api/src/store.rs:292` and re-exported at
`crates/busbar-core/src/governance/mod.rs:948` as `busbar_core::governance::VirtualKey`. The ruling
names the re-export; the definition is the `busbar-api` one. Both die on the same day, because the
`busbar-api` store surface is the shape the retiring engine's store plugins are written against.

Its fields: `id`, `generation_hash`, `name`, `allowed_scopes`, `enabled`, `created_at`, `group`,
`labels`, `expires_at`, `deleted_at`, `revision`, `idp_subject`, `binding_mode`, `minted_by`. Its
two methods: `scope_allowed(kind, value)` (`store.rs:378`) and `is_live()` (`store.rs:392`).

## The finding that shapes the whole cut

**`VirtualKey` carries no budget field, no rate-card reference and no per-key price.** Every one of
the fourteen fields was checked against the struct definition and against every `.field` read in the
tree. There is no amount on it, no cap, no card version and no rate.

What it carries instead is ONE money-ADJACENT field: `group` — "the `groups:` bucket this key
charges through" (`store.rs:312`). That string is not money; it is the NAME of the pot. The money
itself lives at the other end of that name:

- the cap is `GroupCfg.limits[].amount`, projected into
  `busbar_unit_admission::GroupBucket::budget_cap` by `crates/busbar/src/root/policy.rs:315-326`;
- the ledger row it draws from is `group:<group>@<window>[#<kind>:<value>]`, built at
  `crates/busbar/src/root/policy.rs:286-291` (prefix at `policy.rs:211`) and, in the retiring
  engine, at `crates/busbar-core/src/cost.rs:546-550` (prefix at `cost.rs:86`);
- the rate card is keyed by `LaneClass { lane, class }` (`busbar-unit-cost/src/rate.rs`) and by
  model name alone in the retiring `CostModel` (`cost.rs:674` `fn rate_for(&self, model: &str)`) —
  **neither is keyed by a key or by a key's group**. `cost.rs:442` says it outright: "rate_card is
  the ONLY cost source — the 1.4.x pool-member tiered-override loop is GONE."

The struct's own doc says the same thing: "Keys carry NO inline limits: every cap (requests /
tokens / budget / concurrent) lives on the bound group's chain", and
`busbar-substrate/src/config/groups.rs:7`: "Keys are PURE AUTH and carry no limits."

And the proof that the money leg is `group` **and nothing else**:
`crates/busbar-core/src/plane_host/govern.rs:149-192` reconstructs a whole `VirtualKey` from the
ABI's `Facts` populating **only `id` and `group`** — "Only the fields `try_admit`/`chain_for`
actually read are populated… every other field is an inert default `chain_for` never consults",
pinned by `admit_over_facts_matches_try_admit`. Two fields are the entire budget input.

So "the money-carrying fields move to the views" reduces, precisely, to: `group` — the charging-pot
reference and the row identity derived from it. There is no second money field to move, and any
design that assumed one was designing against a struct that does not exist.

### The six headings, totalled

- **IDENTITY** — `id` and `name` are read on the request path (both `principal_from_vkey` sites).
  `idp_subject`, `binding_mode`, `minted_by`, `labels`, `created_at`, `revision` are read ONLY by
  the admin CRUD surface, off the stored row, never off a resolved key. `generation_hash` is
  secret-equivalent (`store.rs:294-300`, redacted in `Debug`) and is read only inside
  `verify_token`'s own rotation check.
- **SCOPE** — `allowed_scopes`, through `scope_allowed` / `pool_allowed`. **Thirteen production
  call sites across six crates**, and two independent `PoolView` traits
  (`busbar-unit-trust/src/guard.rs:62`, `busbar-llm/src/unit/verify.rs:141`) that exist only
  because the resolved key had nowhere to put a scope. This is the one enforced field beyond
  `id`/`name`.
- **BUDGET** — **no field**. The cap is config, projected to `GroupBucket::budget_cap`. What IS
  read off the key is `group`, at seven production sites: `cost.rs:809` (`chain_for`, the one that
  matters), `appbuild.rs:1173`, `mcp/method.rs:2369` and `a2a/receive.rs:1094,1367` (into the ABI
  `Facts`), and — as a tap LABEL and not a limit — `llm/pipeline.rs:277,554,1130,1247` and
  `llm/route.rs:423`. `voice/mount.rs:718,1351` reaches the same chain through `budget_state(key)`.
- **RATE CARD** — **no field**. The card is keyed by `LaneClass`, never by a key or a group.
- **EXPIRY** — `expires_at`. PB-92 says it is unenforced, and that is true **of the keys arm**: the
  arm reads only the token's own `exp`. It is NOT true of the tree. Three production sites refuse
  on it — `substrate/egress_auth/gate.rs:217`, `substrate/trust/validate.rs:339`, and
  `validate.rs:562` (the per-frame `Standing` re-resolve) — each spelling the identical triple
  `!is_live() || !enabled || expires_at.is_some_and(|exp| now >= exp)`. So expiry has a real,
  enforcing reader; what it does not have is an enforcing reader on the KEYS ARM, and moving it
  onto the resolved key must not create one.
- **REVOCATION** — `enabled` + `deleted_at`/`is_live()`. Enforced inside `verify_token` before a
  key is resolved at all, and re-asked at the three liveness-triple sites above plus
  `plane_host/dispatch.rs:269` (which asks `is_live() && enabled` but, inconsistently, not
  expiry). The three-way duplication of one predicate is the defect the single shape closes.

## What the measurement licenses

1. `ResolvedKey` grows **scope** (`scopes` + `scope_allowed`), because scope is enforced off the key
   at thirteen production sites across six crates, and because the two-string shape's absence of it
   is why two independent `PoolView` traits exist.
2. `ResolvedKey` grows **expiry** (`expires_at`) and **revocation** (`enabled`, `deleted_at`),
   because the liveness triple is a real refusal at three sites, spelled three times. It grows them
   as one predicate, `is_live(now)`, so there is one spelling and not four.
3. `ResolvedKey` does NOT grow `generation_hash` (secret-equivalent, redacted even in `Debug`), nor
   `labels`/`created_at`/`revision`/`idp_subject`/`binding_mode`/`minted_by` — every one of those is
   read off the STORED row by the admin CRUD surface and by no holder of a resolved key.
4. `ResolvedKey` does NOT grow `group`. That is the money leg, and ruling 6 sends it to the views.
   Its two halves split exactly as ruling 5 says: the ROW identity (`group:<group>@<window>`)
   belongs to `busbar-unit-ledger`; the ARITHMETIC over that row's cap belongs to
   `busbar-unit-cost`. No money vocabulary enters the auth unit.
