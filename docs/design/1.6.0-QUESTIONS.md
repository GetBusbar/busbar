# QUESTIONS — the queue, read by the owner at the end

**This session runs unattended. Nothing here was asked; everything here was queued.**

Rule 1.8 of `docs/design/1.6.0-KICKOFF.md`: when a slot hits something only the owner can decide,
it appends one row here, marks the item BLOCKED, and **moves on**. The run does not stop.

## What belongs here, and what does not

**Queue it** only when it genuinely needs the owner's authority:

- a **money figure changed** — the PARK file is the evidence, this is the flag (§8.7)
- a **customer-visible behaviour change** that no ruling covers (§9.5)
- a **cost** you were not authorised to incur
- a **phase cannot exit** and you have drained everything else (§1.12)

**Do not queue** a choice you can make yourself. If the code, the spec or an existing ruling
answers it, that is not a question — decide, record the assumption in your slot-log row, and keep
going (§1.10). A queue full of things the reader would answer with "you could have looked" is a
queue nobody finishes reading, and the real question gets lost in it.
