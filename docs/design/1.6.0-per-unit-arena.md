# busbar 1.6.0 — the per-unit arena (design note, slot A1)

**What it is.** The one resource a plugin call is given: 4 KiB of scratch (`busbar_contract::ARENA_BYTES`)
plus a small typed region for span-table pairs (`MAX_KEYS` of them), owned by the task that runs ONE unit,
for the length of that unit. It is opened after the unit's frame has arrived and before the plane is
asked what the frame means; nothing on the hot path allocates after that open. It is bounded: an
allocation that does not fit is refused with the contract's own `ArenaBudget`, which the loop already
maps to `ReasonCode::ArenaBudget` at the step that asked. It is reset at unit end by being DROPPED —
no `reset(&mut self)`, because reclaiming a slice a plane may still hold is what the borrow checker
refuses and rightly; per-frame reuse on a relay path is one fresh space per frame, same stack bytes.

**Who owns it, measured.** The kernel's step seam hands a unit `teller::UnitCtx` (key, origin, session,
generation, two flags) and NO arena; `VoiceUnit::decode` says in its own body that "the plane already
read the frame" before the loop ran. So the plane call sits ABOVE the loop, in whatever drives a unit
(the root's one-shot `LoopDriver`, the root's `SessionLoopDriver`), and that driver is the owner: it
puts an `ArenaSpace` on its own stack per unit, carves a `UnitArena` from it, builds the contract `Ctx`
around it, calls the plane, runs the loop, encodes, and lets the space drop. The brief's parenthetical
("the kernel teller opens it") is not what the seam does today, and this slot does not move the seam.

**The contract face.** `busbar_contract::bounded::Arena`: `&'a self` allocators answering borrows of
`self`, fallible, plus `remaining()`. The shape stays. ONE clause goes: `Send + Sync` becomes `Send`.
Proof of necessity, under `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` on every crate in the tree: a `Sync` implementor
must write through `&self` into storage and lend a `&'a [u8]` out of it. A `Mutex`/`RwLock` cannot —
a borrow cannot escape its guard. Atomics give no `&[u8]` view. `OnceLock` slots can lend, but heap-
allocate per call and cannot type the pair region by the caller's `'a`: `alloc_spans` stores
`(&'a str, Span)` and a field cannot name a method's lifetime. So under `Sync` every implementor is a
double that leaks (`Box::leak`) to fake a lifetime it cannot produce — which is the tree's measured
state, seventeen times. `Sync` bought nothing: `Ctx<'u>` is one unit's, on one task, and nothing shares
an arena while a borrow of it is alive. The one consumer that read `Ctx: Sync` is the egress route
walk's boxed future (`ports::BoxFut`), whose `Send` was only ever true by that bound; it says so now.

**The shipping implementor.** In the ROOT (`crates/busbar/src/root/arena.rs`; no ceiling), because the
owner is the root's driver and the kernel's step seam carries no arena. `ArenaSpace<'u>` is the storage
(two arrays, zeroed once at open); `UnitArena<'u>` holds the unconsumed tail of each region in a `Cell`,
takes it out, splits the head off, puts the tail back — the head is owned by the allocating call at the
moment it is split, which is what lets it be lent as a borrow of the space with nothing unsafe said.
A refusal costs nothing: the tail goes back untouched. The kernel's own `arena::Arena` (span-based,
`&mut self`, `reset` counter) is the loop's relay-scratch shape and is used by no serving path; it is
left alone — folding the two is a kernel decision with a kernel raise, not this slot's.

**What it is NOT.** Not a global allocator, not a pool, not shared across units or threads, not a
plane-visible type beyond the trait (`Ctx::arena()` answers `&dyn Arena`; a plane never sees a name).

**Every current implementor, and what happens to it.** Root: `tests/units_mcp.rs::CellArena` and
`tests/units_voice.rs::CellArena` (leaking) — REPLACED by the shipping `UnitArena`, so the decode cells
drive the step that can actually run out. Contract: `tests/object_safety.rs::NoArena` (refusing double)
stays; `object_safety.rs::LeakArena` and `tests/bounded_limits.rs::LeakArena` stay as proof fixtures;
a `CursorArena` guard is added so the `Sync` clause cannot return without a red. Plane crates
(`plane-admin` `TestArena`/`TinyArena`, `plane-mcp` ×2, `plane-a2a` ×2, `plane-voice` `LeakArena`,
`plane-llm` `LeakArena`), transports (`transport-http` `TestArena`, `transport-stdio` `TestArena`) and
`unit-egress` `LeakArena`: UNCHANGED. They are test doubles inside kinds that may not name the root or
the kernel; a shared test arena would be a kind crossing. Nothing shipping implemented the trait before.

**What this unblocks / does not.** Unblocks: a driver building a real `Ctx` (driver → plane), and with
it `open_upstream`, the tool moat and the duplex answer channel, whose only stated blocker was "no arena
that ships". Does not: `ProductionUnits`' non-admin refusals — those are a COMPOSITION gate (no plane's
units are composed there; `VoiceUnit` is its own `Units`), not an arena gate; the frame never reaches
`Units::decode` by the seam's own shape. Said here so nobody looks for the fix in the wrong place.
