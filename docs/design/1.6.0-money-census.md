**Measured 2026-09-22 on `consolidated/1.6.0`.** Scope: every crate in the workspace, `xtask/`,
and the gates. The standard is the owner's: *treat it like a bank auditor. Would it pass?*

## THE HEADLINE — would a bank auditor pass this?

**No.** Three findings are enough on their own, and each was proven by following the data flow to
the multiply or to the durable write, not by reading a field name:

1. **A computed rate-card price is sealed into the WAL** (V0a). `reserved` is
   `ceil((fee + Σ quantity × unit_price) × tier / 10 000)` written into a hash-chained frame that no
   later rate row can move. #77(3) says price is never stored.
2. **Every configured price in every deployment is quantized through an `f64`, and a family of
   exactly-typed rates comes out one nano-unit light** (V1). `nano_rate(0.5005)` is `500`; the rule
   #44 specifies gives `501`. Proven twice — by the tree's own `D7` test and independently here.
3. **The sanctioned #79 repair path is not wired** (V3). `adjusting_entries` / `recompute` /
   `record_repricing` have **zero production callers**, so a signed back-dated correction moves the
   invoice and never the book.

Two structural findings explain how all of it survived: the `no-float-money` gate scans four money
files and exempts the only one of them with a float, so it is green over ~4 of a ~17-file money
surface (V5); and the fee row cannot see the third spelling of the fee (V13).

What is genuinely right is worth saying too: the store ABI is counts-only and the compiler enforces
it; `BudgetCell` has no spend field at all; `/admin/usage` really does price each row against the
dated history (the spec's "owed work" line under #79 is stale); `Money` and `Count` are exact
`i128`-at-scale-6 types with no float in them; and the live LLM settlement resolves the card at the
posting's own `arrived_ms`.

## V0a — THE DURABLE WAL STORES A COMPUTED RATE-CARD PRICE, SEALED INTO A HASH CHAIN

**Law: #77(3)** — *"Price is NEVER stored — money is a read-time conversion."* **#71** — *"The

`crates/busbar/src/root/durability.rs:511-520`:

`Durability::settle_posted`).

**`reserved` is `Σ count × rate`, traced and verified:**

| Step | Site | Arithmetic |
|---|---|---|
| the rate enters | `units_a2a.rs:1107-1122` | `max_unit_price_nanos: self.bindings.bytes_nanos`; `fee_nanos: pricer.price_per_request_cents() × NANOS_PER_CENT`. Same shape at `units_mcp.rs:837-855`, `units_voice.rs:1305-1315` |

**RECOMMENDATION (PARKED, P0a).** Either (a) journal the hold's INPUTS — the per-class quantities,
the tier and the card seq — and let recovery re-derive the figure by lookup, which restores #77(3)
and costs one multiply on a cold path; or (b) sign an explicit #77(3) carve-out for the recovery
figure, narrow to `reserved`, recorded in Part 2. Do not leave it unsigned.

## V6 — SERVED MONEY BYTES ARE CAST THROUGH `as f64`

**Law: #77(8).** `busbar-kernel/src/metrics.rs`:

instrument, so a hold that omits a charged component is an enforcement gap, not a billing one.

## V-DISC-1 — SUPERSEDED BY V0a / V0b

This entry was opened as *"is the journal's `reserved`/`settled`/`overdraft` a stored price, or a

balance sheet was carrying a computed price, and only following the data flow to the multiply showed
it.** A field-name census would have passed it.

## V-DISC-2 — `cost_per_mtok` / `blended_per_mtok`: an `f64` derived from the rate card, on the wire

`(input + output) / 2.0` (`busbar-substrate-values/src/billing.rs:188`), serialized as `f64` on the

(`api/src/hooks.rs:146`), ranked by the `cheapest` policy (`hooks-ranking/src/lib.rs:168`).

**Judgment: MONEY-ADJACENT, not a billed byte — report, do not fix under #77(8).** It is a
**routing** scalar: no bill is computed from it and no ledger row carries it. #42 contemplates
exactly this reader (*"`cheapest` with billing off: all candidates read 0 → tie → cost hook is a
no-op"*). The honest residual risk is second-order: a rate-derived comparison decided on an ULP can
flip which lane serves, and which lane serves changes the bill. That is a real but indirect effect,
and making it exact is a much smaller change than making it a #77(8) finding would imply.

## V-DISC-3 — `duration_seconds_to_wire(Count) -> f64`

`busbar-substrate-values/src/billing.rs:108-111` converts the exact `Count` back to a double for the
echoed JSON usage object, because `serde_json::Value` cannot hold a decimal string without
`arbitrary_precision` workspace-wide.

**Judgment: the weakest of the exemptions, but defensible today.** Byte-identity is MEASURED, not
hoped — `tests/billing_duration_tests.rs:41-47` compares `to_bits()` of `micros / 1e6` against
parsing the original decimal text over 200 009 values and finds zero differences. It is a
**rendering boundary**, structurally the same shape as #44's card-build exemption. It becomes a
finding the moment a quantity exists that the scale can hold and the double cannot — i.e. the moment
V9 lands and counts genuinely carry fractions.

# 5. THE ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION — is a legacy ENGINE crate holding already-priced nanodollars lawful?

**The subject.** `busbar-voice/src/runtime/metering.rs` (~44 money symbols) plus
`voice_d2_billing_oracle.rs` (~14) and `mount.rs`/`topology/mod.rs`/`runtime/mod.rs` (~15). The
symbols are `estimate_nanos`, `fee_nanos`, `cap_nanos`, `reserved_nanos`, `settled_nanos` —
nanodollars, as the module header says: *"a reserve-then-settle lease over ALREADY-PRICED
nanodollars."* `xtask/src/gates/plane_pricing_blindness.rs`'s `PLANE_CRATES` lists `busbar-voice`,
so the gate scans it; `qa/construction.toml` files it under *"LEGACY ENGINE crates"* and warns *"a
reader must not take either as the plane kind"* — the plane is `busbar-plane-streaming`, which is
clean.

**The answer is: the DIRECTION is lawful; the LOCATION is not; and the crate's status as an engine
rather than a plugin is what makes it debt rather than a breach.** Four separate readings, each
cited:

**(a) #43's letter is NOT broken *by this crate*, and this matters** — though the ABI it speaks is
broken by V0c, which is a finding against the ABI and not against voice. #43 bans a plane plugin *seeing its rate card
or fees*: *"A plane plugin NEVER sees its rate card or fees."* It does not. `LocalLease::price_usage`
holds no rates, no labels and no units and says so at `metering.rs:269-275`; the real pricing rides
`HostLease::price_usage` → `MeteringHost::price_usage` → core's `CostModel::price_usage_nanos`
(`busbar-kernel/src/cost.rs:694`). What crosses the ABI is a `u64` scalar core computed. The
reserved config sub-keys (`rate_card`, `fees`) never cross. **Core prices; the engine receives a
number.** That is Part 1 Law 6 as written — *"Core captures the quantities and prices them against
the rate card the user registered"* — and #77(1) holds too: `CostHold` and `LocalLease` carry no
plane field, so per-plane pricing stays unrepresentable.

**(b) A money hold is not merely permitted, it is REQUIRED by two rows.** #77(6): *"Budgets enforced
over any operator window … per key/group via in-memory holds released when the line is written."*
#77(7): *"`on_exhaustion` = finish-unit (default) | cut-stream — a cut is NOT a refusal (#62): it
settles a closed Abort arm carrying class + **cap** + delivered-qty."* A cut-stream at the budget
ceiling is **impossible without a live money ceiling on the serving path**: something must compare
spend to a cap while the audio is flowing. So the D2 lease is not an unlawful invention; it is the
mechanism #77(7) presupposes. Deleting it would break a shipped guarantee.

**(c) But #71's PERF INVARIANT is broken, flatly and by construction.** #71: *"The MONEY VIEW
computes `Σ count × rate` only at READ time, in the kernel — **never on the serving path**"* and
*"(b) **no rate lookup / multiply / currency math runs on the request path**."* Voice calls
`price_usage` **per turn, mid-session** — a rate lookup and a multiply, on the request path, by
design. That is the violation, and it does not depend on who owns the pricer or which crate the code
sits in. It is the same violation whether the call is made from `busbar-voice` or from the root.

**(d) And #77(2) is in tension, unresolved.** *"ONE sealed FACTS line per unit, written ONCE at the
END — never edited; **no early accrual**, no refunds, no adjusting lines."* Voice settles an exact
increment **per turn**. Whether that is early accrual turns entirely on whether a *unit* is a
session or a turn — a definition the spec does not fix for a live carrier, and the one question in
this whole census I cannot answer from the documents.

**(e) #43's END STATE is the clause that actually condemns the code:** *"ONE kernel-side
implementation of accrual, provenance and keying; a plane emits counts per its declared class **and
nothing else**."* A reserve/settle lease with its own saturating accrual, its own exhaustion
predicate and its own hard-close guard is a second implementation of accrual, living outside the
kernel. #43 names this exact failure mode and its cause: *"The duplication is the defect generator
and today proved it."*

**So: lawful as a mechanism, unlawful as a home, and the tree already agrees.**
`qa/construction.toml` calls these symbols *"real money and real DEBT"* and routes them to
`plane-pricing-blindness`'s burndown rather than to `plane-no-money`'s `max_hits = 0`, on the
explicit reasoning that *"counting them a second time under a `max_hits = 0` with no burndown would
not fix one of them."* That routing is correct and this census does not disturb it.

**RECOMMENDATION (P11).** Do not delete the lease and do not re-home the crate. Move the **decision**
behind the ABI: the host already owns `CostHold` (`busbar-kernel/src/plane/cost.rs`,
`plane_host/cost_host.rs:80-166`) and already answers `cost_settle` with a `LeaseState`. Make the
engine's serving loop consume only that `LeaseState` — `Live` or `Exhausted` — and hold no
`cap_nanos`, no `settled_nanos` and no `fee_nanos` of its own. `HostMeteringPort`/`HostLease` is
already that shape; `LocalLease` is the in-process twin that keeps the numbers plane-side, and the
module's own header concedes it (*"It holds the budget cell in-process rather than in the host's
global registry (the reported neutral-seam gap)"*). Retiring `LocalLease` in favour of a host lease
in every build removes ~44 of the symbols and leaves the engine with a boolean, which is what #43's
"and nothing else" asks for. #71(b) would still be violated by the per-turn multiply; that one needs
the owner's ruling on (d) before it can be designed away.
