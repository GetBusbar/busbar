## ⏸ Queued for owner — money-adjacent / ABI (NOT auto-fixed)
- **busbar-contract — 2 HIGH #40 secret-accessor seal.** `SecretValue::expose() -> &[u8]` (kinds.rs:439) and `KeyMaterial.bytes` public field (kinds.rs:104) are raw-secret accessors reachable on the plugin-facing ABI, enforced only by out-of-tree grep, not sealed like `TransportKeyHandle`. Recommend: gate `expose()`/`bytes` behind a `KernelSeal` (crate-private read token) so a plugin literally has no symbol to obtain bytes. ABI change — touches secret/auth plugins + kernel. **This is the #40 "make it impossible in code" gap.**
- **busbar-unit-audit — 2 HIGH + 4 MED + 1 LOW (money-path audit chain).** resume_missing/resume_scoped silently swallow `ChainBreak` on runtime evict-then-resume (tamper evidence permanently lost); `What::destination`/`Access::fields`/`Adjust.*` free-text unredacted onto the erasure-exempt chain; global positions lock held across durable I/O (throughput); record_by ts/seq monotonicity race. Folds → `busbar-kernel-audit`. Fix on the consolidated crate under oracle.
- **transport-tcp OPEN — listener prune.** `self.listeners` never pruned; the `Transport` trait exposes no `unlisten`/close-listener op. Needs a contract/ABI decision (add the op to `busbar-contract`).
- **plane-streaming — HIGH lane mis-attribution (money-adjacent).** With >1 configured upstream, `verify()` reports `upstreams().first().lane` not the dialed upstream's lane → units metered/ceiling'd against the wrong lane. Fix needs care (metering). Queued.
